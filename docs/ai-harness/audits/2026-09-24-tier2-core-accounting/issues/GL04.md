## Problem statement

createReversal copies inverted lines and source lineage but does not claim the original or establish an enforced original-to-reversal relationship. The service assigns a fresh slip on each invocation, and the original remains POSTED.

## Reproduction / evidence

P04 successfully creates two independent reversing journals from the same original. Both can independently enter the normal approval/posting path. The original's preserved POSTED state is not itself wrong: the defect is absence of an exactly-once reversal control. Simply changing it to REVERSED would also affect current POSTED-only aggregation and needs a deliberate accounting policy.

Executed diagnostic observations: `P04` in [probe-results.json](../evidence/probe-results.json); source: [AuditProbes.java](../evidence/AuditProbes.java). Probe PASS confirms the observation, not remediation.

## Financial / architectural impact

Repeated retries or concurrent cancellation requests can over-reverse the economic event and distort balances.

## Proposed solution

Persist a unique reversal operation linked to the original and define its pending/posted/cancelled lifecycle. Keep original historical amounts intact and make repeated requests return the same operation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Sequential and concurrent duplicate reversal commands result in at most one effective reversal.
- [ ] Copy transaction/base amounts and inverted sides exactly; validate supplied reversal accounting date against the target open period.
- [ ] Rebuilds include original and effective reversal consistently; a failed or abandoned draft does not permanently consume the reversal right.
- [ ] Add regression coverage that fails on the audited version and passes after remediation; preserve existing financial controls.
