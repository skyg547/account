## Problem statement

Posting rechecks the period before mutation, but the check does not reserve admission or hold a lock shared with final close. A close can complete between this read and the journal transaction's commit.

## Reproduction / evidence

Static interleaving: A reads OPEN at PostingService:52; B finalizes CLOSED; A commits its journal/entries/balances. Master period writes do lock their own row, but the Journal read/commit is not coordinated with that lock. Existing documentation explicitly acknowledges this limitation.

Evidence level: source and call-path analysis. The described failure schedule is a reproduction plan unless explicitly identified as executed above.

## Financial / architectural impact

Transactions can enter a period after closing checks and final cutoff have completed.

## Proposed solution

Use an admission/fencing protocol shared with closing: drain in-flight postings and advance a durable period epoch or equivalent atomic gate before close finalization.
