## Problem statement

An existing deterministic annual slip with a matching date/description/type causes immediate return. Financial lines and source snapshot are not compared. A reopened year with an existing posted closing slip has the same short-circuit.

## Reproduction / evidence

P17 generates a1000 annual draft, changes source revenue to1500, and reruns. Only one command exists and retained earnings stays1000. The probe uses the real service with controlled query/posting ports.

Executed diagnostic observations: `P17` in [probe-results.json](../evidence/probe-results.json); source: [AuditProbes.java](../evidence/AuditProbes.java). Probe PASS confirms the observation, not remediation.

## Financial / architectural impact

Temporary accounts and retained earnings can remain incompletely closed while rerun reports success.

## Proposed solution

Freeze and identify the closing source snapshot; reject or safely replace stale drafts, and support an auditable delta/reclose operation after reopening.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Changed source amounts, accounts or classifications cannot reuse an obsolete annual draft silently.
- [ ] Posted annual close plus reopened-year adjustments yields a controlled delta closing.
- [ ] Validate full content/lineage and posted completeness, not only a slip header.
- [ ] Add regression coverage that fails on the audited version and passes after remediation; preserve existing financial controls.

## Test gap and verification limits

Existing stable-retry tests do not change underlying annual activity.

The six requested module suites passed 369 tests, but their success does not establish this missing control. No production data, live PostgreSQL, distributed fault injection or production load testing was used for this audit. A remediation must supply the relevant additional evidence before this issue is closed.
