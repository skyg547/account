## Problem statement

Absent explicit dates, the helper defaults to yesterday at execution time. Cleanup and the step-scoped reader resolve independently, and a restarted JobInstance retains its completed cleanup and reader offset.

## Reproduction / evidence

Static schedule: run without date parameters, fail after cleanup/one chunk, restart on the next day. Reader targets a different day using the old checkpoint while cleanup remains completed. Midnight between step initialization can produce the same mismatch.

Evidence level: source and call-path analysis. The described failure schedule is a reproduction plan unless explicitly identified as executed above.

## Financial / architectural impact

One day remains missing/partial and another day can be skipped or double aggregated.

## Proposed solution

Require immutable identifying start/end dates, or resolve once before work and persist/reuse them for every step and restart.
